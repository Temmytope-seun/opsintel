# Change management

- **Standard changes** are pre-approved and follow a documented procedure.
- **Normal changes** need CAB approval, a tested rollback plan and a maintenance window. Firmware upgrades to customer-facing network equipment are always normal changes.
- **Emergency changes** may be made to restore service during an incident. They must be approved by the on-call manager and retrospectively reviewed by CAB within 2 working days.
- About one in eight incidents in a typical quarter is change-related; every change-related P1/P2 requires a CAB review.
