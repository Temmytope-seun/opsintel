# Cooling alarm response

1. Confirm the alarm against the BMS: supply air temperature and CRAC unit status.
2. If one CRAC unit has failed and the room is within ASHRAE limits (18-27 C), raise a P3 and call the maintenance contractor.
3. If inlet temperature exceeds 27 C at any rack, raise a P1, start the Major Incident process and notify colocation customers in the affected hall.
4. Never switch off customer equipment; customers must be contacted to shed load.
