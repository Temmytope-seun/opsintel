# Major Incident Management

A major incident is any P1, or any P2 affecting more than five customer sites or a Gold-tier customer's headquarters.

1. The service desk declares the major incident and pages the on-call Major Incident Manager (MIM).
2. The MIM opens a bridge within 15 minutes and assigns a technical lead and a communications lead.
3. Customer updates every 30 minutes (P1) or 60 minutes (P2) until resolution.
4. Within 5 working days, a post-incident review is held and a problem record is raised if the root cause is not fully remediated.
5. Recurring incidents with the same root cause (3+ in 30 days) must be linked to a single problem record.
