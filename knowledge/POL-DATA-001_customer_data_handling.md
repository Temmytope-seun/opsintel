# Customer data handling and AI tooling

- Customer contact details (names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers) are personal data under UK GDPR. They must not be exported from the CRM/CMDB into reports, chat tools or AI assistants.
- AI decision-support tools may use operational data (tickets, alerts, costs) and must operate on pseudonymised customer identifiers.
- Any action an AI tool recommends that affects customers, spend or production systems requires approval by an accountable human manager. The tool must record the evidence it relied on.
- Requests for bulk personal data must go to the DPO.
